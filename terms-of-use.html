Terms of Use

The terms for using Propana and taking the funded challenge. Read them alongside our Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: 28-09-2026

These Terms of Use govern your access to this site and to the Propana funded trading challenge (the “Service”). By connecting a wallet, paying a challenge fee or otherwise using the Service, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

The Service

Propana runs a trading evaluation. You pay a one-time fee in SOL and receive a simulated account that trades against live Solana market prices. Orders are simulated: no tokens are bought or sold on your behalf, and challenge balances are not money and cannot be withdrawn.

If you pass every stage of the challenge you may be offered a funded account, on the terms shown on the site at that time, including the profit share and payout schedule.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and legally able to use the Service where you live. You may not use the Service if you are subject to sanctions or if doing so would break the law in your jurisdiction. One live account per person.

Fees

The challenge fee is quoted in US dollars and paid in SOL at the rate shown when you start checkout. The fee is paid on the Solana blockchain directly to the Propana treasury and is final once confirmed. Fees are not refunded when an account breaches a rule.

A payment is only credited once it is confirmed on chain from the wallet you signed in with, to the treasury address shown, for at least the quoted amount.

Rules

Each account is bound by the rules published on the site, including stage profit targets, the drawdown floor, minimum trading activity, the tradeable-token gate and the per-token profit cap. Rules are enforced automatically by our servers on every order, and the server’s record is final.

Market data comes from third-party sources. Fills include a fee and a slippage model. We may refuse to quote a token whose market is too thin, too new or priced inconsistently.

Prohibited Conduct

You may not manipulate prices of tokens you trade, exploit bugs or data errors, run multiple accounts, share or sell accounts, automate trading against the Service without permission, or attempt to access other users’ data. We may void results, close accounts and withhold payouts that result from prohibited conduct.

Payouts

Payouts on funded accounts are made in SOL to the wallet you signed in with, subject to the payout rules shown on the site, including minimum amounts, cooldowns and review of large requests. We may delay a payout while we investigate suspected abuse.

Risk and No Advice

Nothing on the Service is investment, financial, legal or tax advice. Digital assets are volatile, and blockchain transactions cannot be reversed. You are responsible for your wallet, your keys and the transactions you sign.
